01

The scenario

Read the context and success criteria before you analyze the data.

A grocery delivery company is testing a new mobile shopping app. A UX research team observed 24 participants using the app on their phones. Each participant was asked to find 12 items from a grocery list, add the correct quantities to their cart, apply a discount code, select a delivery window, and complete a simulated checkout. All participants eventually completed the task, but their completion times, errors, and satisfaction ratings differed.

Use the results to decide whether the mobile app meets its usability goals and explain the limits of your conclusions.

Goal 01 · EfficiencyAverage task timeAt most 30 minutes
Goal 02 · On-time completionParticipants within targetAt least 80% finish in 30 minutes
Goal 03 · RobustnessAverage number of errorsAt most 2 errors per participant
Goal 04 · SatisfactionAverage satisfaction ratingAt least 4 out of 5

Questions 7–10
